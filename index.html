Welcome to the Smart Campus Portal

Course details, faculty information, announcements and deadlines used to live in different places. The portal brings them together so you spend less time searching and more time studying.

  • See every registered course and its instructor.
  • Know where to be, and when, with the weekly timetable.
  • Plan assignments with a task list that remembers your progress.
  • Switch to dark mode for late-night study sessions.
